# Legal Content Guide

## Rules
- Sources: Pakistan Code (pakistancode.gov.pk), National Assembly site, official police/legal-aid pages. Record source_url on every card.
- Write in plain words, steps as checklist, max about 150 words per card.
- Every card has last_reviewed and a reviewer name. Mark unverified cards "draft" and do not demo them as fact.
- Do not copy long legal text; summarize in your own words.
- Do not promise outcomes. Add "may" where the law has exceptions.
- Emergency numbers: verify before release (Police Emergency 15 is widely published, e.g. on Islamabad and Punjab police sites).

## Starter topics (target 25 cards x 3 languages if time; minimum 12 English + Roman)
Police stop, arrest/detention, FIR registration, FIR refused, bail basics, harassment at work, street harassment, domestic violence help, online fraud, hacked account/cyber harassment, consumer complaint, tenant/landlord issue, unpaid salary, property possession dispute, how to find legal aid, what to save as evidence.

## Starting points to verify (do NOT trust blindly)
The Constitution contains fundamental-rights provisions on security of person, safeguards on arrest and detention (being told the grounds, consulting a lawyer of choice, production before a magistrate within a fixed period) and dignity. Read the exact articles on Pakistan Code and cite them in each card. A law student or lawyer should review before the demo.
